Sandun Dassanayake

Senior Lecturer, Department of Decision Sciences, University of Moratuwa

Geographic Information Science, Remote Sensing, Soft Computing

Public CV Google Scholar LinkedIn

I conduct spatial analysis and research related to decision sciences using tools like GIS, remote sensing, and machine learning. My overlapping interdisciplinary research interests have mainly contributed to sustainable urban planning practices, geohazard predictions, and optimized geo-environmental design to combat climate change. The three of my most cited papers include a paper on probabilistic stability evaluation for wildlife-damaged earth dams, pavement texture and color effects on Urban Heat Islands, and floating wetland remediation of Acid Mine Drainage. The collaborative paper on peak shear strength along soil-geocomposite drainage layer interfaces using hybrid artificial intelligence approaches demonstrates my passion for using advanced computational methods to improve geotechnical analysis and design. I am intrigued by the environmental remediation techniques, such as floating wetlands for Acid Mine Drainage treatment, and the potential of nature-based water purification and ecological restoration techniques. Also, my mining and civil engineering background has supported me in further investigating the climate change-induced physical phenomena experienced by geoengineered structures using machine learning and statistical modeling. These research attempts mainly contribute to narrowing the gap in our understanding of predicting geohazards and risk.
