Prompts for LLMs

  • Make a list of propositions in this text in the format “X is a type of Y”, “W is caused by X”, “A explains B”. Put it into a table with three columns.
  • Give me a multi level explanation of momentum. First at the level of a child, then a high school student and then an academic explanation.
  • Act as a Socratic tutor and help me understand the concept of momentum in physics. Ask me questions to guide my understanding.
